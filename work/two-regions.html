Two regions, one fleet

One login for the world, data that stays in its own region, and a robot's whole history moves when the robot does.

A simplified, general version of how this works. No company code, customer names or internal details.

The problem

The fleet grew outside India, so the platform now runs in two AWS regions, India and the US. Customers should sign in once from anywhere, while their data stays in their own region. Robots also move between regions, and their history has to move with them.

How it works

Each region is a self contained cell with its own API and database. Login is global. A thin routing layer in front looks up where each customer lives and sends every request to the right cell. Moving a customer is a copy job between cells that checks everything first, tracks each item, and leaves an audit trail.

What I built

  • The routing layer on AWS Lambda with a DynamoDB lookup.
  • The transfer: checks before anything moves, a status for every item so a rerun never copies twice, and an audit trail.
  • A transfer wizard in the dashboard, so ops can move a customer without a script.
  • Most of the infrastructure code: Terraform for each cell and the rollout scripts.

Choices that mattered

  • Cells over one global database. A problem in one region stays in that region, and data stays where the customer is.
  • Transfers you can safely rerun. Every item records its own status, so a half finished run just continues.
  • Everything in Terraform. A new region is mostly configuration, not a long list of console clicks.
